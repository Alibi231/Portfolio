Life Rocks!

Itch.io Download

A first-person stone skipper made for Sheridan's 2026 winter game design Week (March 2nd-6th, 2026). The design challenge was making a game around a unique verb, with my team choosing "skipping". The game is a sandbox where the player times their throws to skip rocks at targets and go for a high score. I worked on both the game assets and C# programming in Unity.
