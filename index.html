Life Rocks!

Itch.io Download

A first-person stone skipper made for Sheridan's 2026 winter game design Week (March 2nd-6th, 2026). The design challenge was making a game around a unique verb, with my team choosing "skipping". The game is a sandbox where the player times their throws to skip rocks at targets and go for a high score. I worked on both the game assets and C# programming in Unity.



Enjoy The Launch

Twine File Download

Enjoy The Launch is an ARG developed within the span of a month in Sheridan's year one Play Design class. The design challenge was making an ARG within a randomly assigned group of four, that centered around equity. Our's centered around climate change, and a narrative that the government was sending the rich to a different planet after learning that the Earth was too far gone. The trailhead was made entirely by me, which meant learning about how Twine - and by extension HTML - works and how to use it, including learning how to make a functioning search bar for players to type their guesses into. I also made the games opening puzzle, which involves Using ASCII to convert out of place numbers into a keyword that can be plugged into the aforementioned search bar.



Grade School Gumshoe

Grade School Gumshoe is a card game that produced from ideation to polished final product within the span of a semester. Within a group of four, we designed and produced a wallet card game, which is a card game comprising of only eighteen cards. I was a large part of the design of the game, and worked in InDesign to layout the cards and put them in a printable format.



Spacies

Itch.io Download

Spacies is a two player fighting game made entirely in August of 2026. This project was made by two people, myself and a partner that handled the art and provided feedback on the games design. This project demonstrates my ability to work within a deadline, and see a project through all stages of production: ideation, prototyping, production, testing, and polish.
